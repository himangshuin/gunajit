About the Poet

Assam's Voice in
Verse & Song

I'm Gunajit Kalita — an Assamese lyricist, poet, and composer born in Balapara, Bajali district, Assam. Writing and composing since 2012, my pen finds meaning in struggle, love and the stories that live between the lines.

As a lyricist, my words have been sung by Zubeen Garg, Priyanka Bharali, Sanjib Bora, Subasana Dutta, Namrata Kashyap and many leading Assamese singers. As a poet, story writer and social worker, I believe art is never far from service.

About Gunajit

Common questions about the lyricist, his work, and Assamese music.

Gunajit Kalita (Instagram: @gunajit.0) is an Assamese lyricist, poet, composer, story writer and social worker from Balapara, Bajali district, Assam, India. His songs have been sung by Zubeen Garg, Priyanka Bharali, Sanjib Bora, Subasana Dutta, Namrata Kashyap, Deba Geetz and Sanat. He has been writing and composing since 2012.
Gunajit writes primarily in Assamese, with themes of human struggle, love, nature, and the longing of the human soul. His lyric philosophy is captured in his signature line: "সংগ্রামৰ আন এটি নাম জীৱন" — Struggle has another name, that is life.
You can find Gunajit's songs and compositions on his YouTube channel at youtube.com/@gunajitkalita6667 and discover his creative journey through Instagram @gunajit.0, where he regularly shares lyrics, poems, and performance videos.
Yes! Gunajit is open to collaborations with musicians, composers, and fellow artists. Reach him through Instagram DM (@gunajit.0) or via the contact page. He has collaborated on multiple songs and is particularly interested in Assamese music projects.
This Assamese phrase translates to "Struggle has another name — that is Life." It is Gunajit's guiding creative philosophy, reflecting his belief that art and hardship are inseparable, and that beauty is found within the struggle itself.
