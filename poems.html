Fragments from His Verse

সংগ্রামৰ আন এটি নাম জীৱন।

Struggle has another name — that name is life.

আকাশৰ গাত লাগি বোৱা তুমি।

You flow, clinging to the fabric of the sky.

তুমি মেঘ বৰষীয়া চাদৰ।

You are the cloud-drenched sheet of rain.

আকুল হৃদয়ে বিচাৰে তোমাক।

The restless heart searches only for you.

প্ৰতিটো শব্দ এটি প্ৰাৰ্থনা।

Every word is a prayer in disguise.

নিঃশব্দতাতো আছে এটি গান।

Inside silence, there lives a song.

On Writing Poetry

Why Gunajit
Writes in Assamese

For Gunajit, writing in Assamese is not a choice — it is a necessity. The Assamese language carries centuries of philosophical and poetic tradition. Words like আকুল (restless longing) and সংগ্রাম (struggle) have textures in Assamese that no translation can fully carry.

His poetry draws from the Brahmaputra's currents, from Bihu's rhythm, from the smell of rain on red Assamese soil. These are not decorative references — they are the primary material of thought for a poet who has never needed to look elsewhere for beauty or meaning.

"I write in Assamese because the most honest things I know can only be said in Assamese. Every other language is a translation of the original feeling — and translations always lose something."
