Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working and collaborating with Gunajit.

The fastest way to reach Gunajit is through Instagram DM (@gunajit.0). He typically responds within 1–2 days. For formal project inquiries, email works best and usually receives a reply within 3–5 working days.
Gunajit's primary creative language is Assamese. While he understands and communicates in Hindi and English, his lyrical and poetic work is written in Assamese. If your project requires another language, this can be discussed — but Assamese is where his voice is strongest.
Typically: you share the musical demo, mood references, and thematic intent. Gunajit then writes a first draft, which is reviewed together. Revisions are discussed until the lyric feels right for both parties. Timelines and rates are discussed directly based on the project scope.
Yes — Gunajit is open to cultural events, literary festivals, and music collaborations across Northeast India and beyond, depending on the nature of the event. Reach out with event details and dates for availability and logistics discussion.
Absolutely. Gunajit believes in supporting the next generation of Assamese artists. He is open to mentorship conversations, student projects, and collaborative work with emerging musicians and writers — especially those working in the Assamese language.
