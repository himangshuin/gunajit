Hello, I'm Gunajit 👋

I'm a lyricist, poet, and composer rooted deeply in Assamese culture and language. Writing is not just what I do — it is how I understand the world, how I make sense of struggle, love, and the quiet ordinary things that carry the most meaning.

My words are shaped by Assam — by the Brahmaputra's currents, by the damp smell of rain on red earth, by the rhythm of Bihu and the philosophical weight of Assamese thought. I write in the language of my ancestors, but about feelings that belong to everyone.

"সংগ্রামৰ আন এটি নাম জীৱন।"

— Gunajit Kalita · Personal Philosophy

What I Create 🎵

As a lyricist, I write words for songs — collaborating with musicians and composers to give melody a voice. My lyrics lean into the emotional, the philosophical, and the poetic. They don't just describe; they feel.

As a poet, I write verse that lives outside music too — in Assamese, about the textures of daily life, about longing, about the man sitting with his dog and guitar, waiting for the song to find him.

Beyond the creative world, I serve as a Scientific Officer and social worker — proof that the Assamese soul has always believed that service and art are two sides of the same coin.

Why I Write 🖊️

I write because silence is not neutral. In a world full of noise, a poem is a form of protest. A lyric is a prayer. A song is a hand extended to someone in the dark, saying: I know this feeling. You are not alone in it.

Assamese literature has a rich tradition of poetry and song. I carry that tradition forward, but I also push at its edges — using Assamese to speak of modern anxiety, digital loneliness, and the ancient human need to be truly heard.

Journey

The Gunajit Kalita Story

From a curious writer to Assam's lyrical voice — here's the creative timeline.

Early Years
First Words — Growing Up in Assam
Grew up surrounded by Assamese culture, music, and oral tradition. Discovered early that words had weight — that language was not just communication but ceremony. Began writing poetry privately, finding in verse the only language that felt honest.
Formative
From Poetry to Lyrics
Made the transition from private verse to collaborative songwriting. First lyric commissions for Assamese musicians. Discovered that lyrics live differently than poems — they must survive the breath of a singer, the tempo of a melody, the space between chords.
Growth
Building an Artistic Identity
Developed a distinctive lyrical voice — rooted in Assamese philosophical tradition but emotionally modern. Songs began performing at cultural festivals across Assam. Social media following grew organically around authentic Assamese creative content.
Recent
Aakul Taa & Growing Recognition
Release of Aakul Taa — a song that captured the restless quality of Assamese feeling. Live performances at cultural events including the Habi Festival. Instagram community grew to 494 followers around genuine artistic work.
Today
Continuing to Write, Sing, and Serve
Active as lyricist, poet, social worker, and Scientific Officer. 380+ posts documenting a creative life lived in Assam. Committed to Assamese language and culture — writing for the love of it, the way it has always been done.
What Drives Me

Creative Values

01
Truth Over Beauty
I would rather write an ugly honest lyric than a beautiful hollow one. The words that stay with people are the ones that cost something to write. Authenticity is not a style — it is the minimum requirement.
02
Assamese First
Writing in Assamese is not a limitation — it is a position. The richness of our language, its idioms, its rhythms, its philosophical weight — these are gifts. I write in Assamese because that is where the real words live for me.
03
Struggle as Material
সংগ্রামৰ আন এটি নাম জীৱন. Struggle is not something to be overcome and left behind — it is the material of every worthwhile poem. My work does not look away from difficulty. It makes art from it.
04
Service & Creativity Together
Being a social worker and scientific officer alongside being a poet is not a contradiction. In the Assamese tradition, the intellectual has always served the community. Art and service are the same calling answered differently on different days.
