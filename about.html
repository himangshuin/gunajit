Hello, I'm Gunajit 👋

I'm a lyricist, poet, and composer from Balapara village in the Bajali district of Assam. My pen finds meaning in the raw human struggle, the quiet beauty of Assamese life, and the stories that live between the lines.

Born to Ramakanta Kalita and Bhatima Kalita, I grew up with a deep love for music and poetry. From a very young age I began writing poems — and since 2012, I have been actively involved in music and songwriting. I hold a graduation degree and a qualification in Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT).

Voices That Sing My Words 🎤

Many of Assam's finest playback singers have lent their voices to songs written and composed by Gunajit Kalita. His lyrics reflect both the essence of love and the philosophy of life:

⭐ Zubeen Garg Subasana Dutta Priyanka Bharali Sanjib Bora Deba Geetz Namrata Kashyap Sanat & many more…

Many of the songs and poems written by Gunajit have won the hearts of the people of Assam. He has also been invited as a distinguished guest at numerous cultural events, and his work has been featured in newspapers and media across the region.

What I Create 🎵

As a lyricist, I craft words that singers breathe life into. My lyrics lean into the emotional, the philosophical, and the poetic — they don't just describe, they feel. As a poet, I write verse for the soul that seeks itself — in Assamese, about longing, love, nature, and the everyday extraordinary.

I'm also a story writer and social worker — believing that art and service are two sides of the same human coin. In the Assamese tradition, the intellectual has always served the community.

Why I Write 🖊️

I write because silence is not neutral. A lyric is a prayer. A song is a hand extended to someone in the dark, saying: I know this feeling. You are not alone in it.

Journey

The Gunajit Kalita Story

From Balapara village to Assam's creative stages — a timeline of words, music and service.

Early Years
Born in Balapara, Bajali District
Born to Ramakanta Kalita and Bhatima Kalita in Balapara village, Assam. Growing up surrounded by Assamese culture, music and oral tradition, he discovered early that words had weight — and began writing poetry privately from a young age.
2012
Entering the World of Music
Since 2012, Gunajit has been actively involved in music and songwriting. A deep personal passion grew into a serious creative practice alongside pursuing a graduation degree and Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT) qualification.
Rising
Zubeen Garg & Leading Singers of Assam
His lyrics began reaching the voices of Assam's most celebrated singers — including the legendary Zubeen Garg, Subasana Dutta, Priyanka Bharali, Sanjib Bora, Deba Geetz, Namrata Kashyap and Sanat. Each collaboration brought his words to broader audiences across the state.
Recognition
Featured in Media & Cultural Events
Invited as a distinguished guest at numerous cultural events. His work has been covered in newspapers and media across Assam. His songs and poems have won the hearts of people — recognised not just in musical circles but in the broader cultural life of the state.
Today
50+ Songs, Still Writing
Today Gunajit continues to write, compose, and serve — with over 50 original songs, an active Instagram (@gunajit.0), and a YouTube channel where his music lives. Committed to the Assamese language, to artistic truth, and to the belief that struggle and beauty are inseparable.
What Drives Me

Creative Values

01
Truth Over Beauty
An ugly honest lyric is worth more than a beautiful hollow one. The words that stay with people are the ones that cost something to write. Authenticity is not a style — it is the minimum requirement.
02
Assamese First
Writing in Assamese is not a limitation — it is a position. The richness of our language, its idioms, its rhythms, its philosophical weight are gifts. I write in Assamese because that is where the real words live.
03
Struggle as Material
সংগ্রামৰ আন এটি নাম জীৱন. Struggle is not something to overcome and leave behind — it is the material of every worthwhile poem. My work does not look away from difficulty. It makes art from it.
04
Service & Creativity
Being a social worker alongside being a poet is not a contradiction. In the Assamese tradition, the intellectual has always served the community. Art and service are the same calling answered differently on different days.
Craft

Skills & Specialties

Over a decade of writing in Assamese has built a distinctive creative practice.

✍️
Lyric Writing
Crafting Assamese song lyrics that survive melody, carry emotion, and remain beautiful when read alone — performed by Zubeen Garg, Priyanka Bharali and Assam's finest voices.
📖
Poetry
Long and short form Assamese poetry — verse inhabiting the full emotional range from grief to joy, from the intimate to the philosophical.
🎵
Composition
Writing and composing original songs — developing the melody and lyric together, understanding how words and music breathe as one.
📝
Story Writing
Narrative writing in Assamese — stories that carry the same philosophical depth and emotional honesty as his poetry and lyrics.
🌿
Cultural Storytelling
Drawing on Assamese oral and literary tradition to create work that honours heritage while speaking to the modern world and its struggles.
🤝
Social Work
Active as a social worker in Assam — believing that creative work and community service are inseparable acts of care for the same people.
